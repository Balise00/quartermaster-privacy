Quartermaster privacy policy

Balise Studio · Effective 1 October 2026 · Getting started guide

In short: everything you put into Quartermaster is stored on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking, you don't need an account, and we don't sell or share your data. The only things that leave your phone are the ones you choose yourself: optional connections to Google Calendar, Health Connect and Open Food Facts; anything you put in a household or friends group, which is synced so the other people in that group can see it; and, only if you turn them on, preset suggestions and "Help improve Quartermaster".

What the app stores, and where

Your meals, recipes, stock, meal plans, schedule, projects, habits, gym sessions, swims, weight, goals and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Erase everything" in Settings, deletes it.

Optional connections

Each of these is off until you turn it on, and can be turned off in Settings at any time.

  • Google Calendar. If you sign in with Google, the app reads your calendar events to build your schedule, and adds, changes or deletes the events you plan in the app (for example project tasks and gym sessions). This happens directly between your phone and Google. We never receive your Google data, and it is not used for anything else or shared with anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Health Connect. With your permission, the app reads steps, distance, sleep and weight from Health Connect to show your daily steps, sleep and weight trend. This data stays on your phone. It is never sent anywhere, including to Google Calendar. You can remove access in Health Connect's settings.
  • Food search and barcode lookup. When you search for a food by name, or scan or type a product barcode, the words you typed or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts to look up products and their nutrition. Nothing else is sent with them. Scanning uses Google's on-device barcode scanner, which doesn't need camera permission.
  • Recipe links. When you add a recipe from a link (or share a page to the app), your phone downloads that page from the recipe website to read the ingredients, just as a browser would. Nothing else is sent, and we never see which recipes you look at.
  • Obsidian. If you use the Obsidian version, data is saved in your own vault's plugin folder.

Household & friends groups

This is optional. If you create or join a group, these things are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, run by Google on our behalf) so everyone in the group sees the same thing:

  • the name you choose to show in groups, and a colour;
  • what you add to the group: chores and who did them, the shared shopping list, joint projects and their tasks, and the points they earn;
  • if "Share my weekly numbers" is on for that group: this week's counts of workouts, distance, swims, step-goal days and 1 Thing days, and the same counts for each of the last 31 days so group challenges (such as "most swims in October") can be scored. Nothing else about your health, meals or weight is shared;
  • challenges you set in a group: the title, what's being counted, the dates and any target.

By default you don't sign in for this. Firebase gives the app an anonymous id so it can tell members apart.

Linking to Google (optional). If you tap "Link to Google" (Settings → Your data, or on the Home tab after a reinstall), your anonymous id is linked to your Google account using Firebase Authentication, so you can sign in again on a new install and get your groups back. Firebase then holds your Google email address and account id, and we store a small profile that only you can read: your group name, colour and the list of groups you're in. It isn't used for anything else. "Delete my sharing account" in Settings → Your data leaves your groups and deletes the profile, the anonymous id and the Google link.

Only members of a group can read it, and you join only with a code someone in the group gives you. Turning off "Share my weekly numbers" deletes your numbers from that group. "Leave group" removes you from it; chores and items you added stay for the others. To have everything you added to a group deleted, email us with the group and the name you used in it.

Suggestions and "Help improve Quartermaster" (both optional)

  • Suggest as a preset. When you tap the lightbulb on a habit or saved recipe and confirm, that one habit's name and settings, or that recipe's name, portions, ingredients with grams, macros and web address, are sent to us so we can consider making it a ready-made preset. Nothing you've logged goes with it, and it isn't linked to your name.
  • Help improve Quartermaster. Off unless you turn it on in Settings → Your data, where you can see exactly what would be sent. Once a week it sends: the app version; the names and types of your habits; the names, portions and website of your saved recipes; which features you have switched on; and a few counts (such as how many days you logged in the last four weeks, and how many habits, recipes, batch meals and groups you have). It is stored under a random id that isn't linked to you or your groups. It never includes your meals, weight, health data, schedule or anything you've logged. Turning it off deletes what was sent.

Both are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), can't be read back by the app, and are only used to improve Quartermaster's presets and features.

Deleting your account and data

Quartermaster doesn't need an account. The only account is the optional one behind household and friends groups (an anonymous id, plus your Google email if you linked it).

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my sharing account. This removes you from your groups and deletes your profile, your anonymous id and any Google link straight away.
  • Without the app (for example if you've uninstalled it): email balisestudio.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Quartermaster account" and the Google email you linked, or the name you used in your groups. We'll delete your account, profile and group membership within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Chores, shopping items and other things you added to a group stay for the other members unless you ask us to delete them too.
  • Data saved on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Erase my data" in Settings.
  • "Help improve Quartermaster" data is deleted when you turn it off. Preset suggestions aren't linked to you, so they can't be traced back to you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your phone: when rest between gym sets is over, and any reminders you switch on in Settings (for example to log a meal or close the day). They're scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file of your data and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes (Drive, Files, email…) is up to you. We never receive it.

Children

Quartermaster isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
