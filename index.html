Quartermaster privacy policy

Balise Studio · Effective 28 October 2026 · Getting started guide

In short: everything you put into Quartermaster is stored on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking, you don't need an account, and we don't sell or share your data. The only things that leave your phone are the ones you choose yourself: optional connections to Google Calendar, Google Drive backup and sync, Health Connect and Open Food Facts; live sync between your devices, which keeps a copy of your data in our cloud under your Google account where only you can read it; anything you put in a household or friends group, which is synced so the other people in that group can see it; and, only if you turn them on, preset suggestions and "Help improve Quartermaster".

What the app stores, and where

Your meals, recipes, stock, meal plans, schedule, projects, habits, gym sessions, swims, weight, goals and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Erase everything" in Settings, deletes it.

Optional connections

Each of these is off until you turn it on, and can be turned off in Settings at any time.

  • Google Calendar. If you sign in with Google, the app reads your calendar events to build your schedule, and adds, changes or deletes the events you plan in the app (for example project tasks and gym sessions). This happens directly between your phone and Google. We never receive your Google data, and it is not used for anything else or shared with anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Health Connect. With your permission, the app reads steps, distance, sleep, weight and exercise sessions (workouts) from Health Connect, going back up to 30 days, to show your daily steps, sleep and weight trend and to add workouts from your watch or other apps to your log. It only reads; it never writes to Health Connect. It is saved on your phone, as part of your history. It is never sent to Google Calendar or anyone else, except that if you turn on backup or sync between devices (below), your history, including the daily steps, distance, sleep, weight and workouts the app filed from Health Connect, goes with the rest of your data: to your own Google Drive, or with live sync to your own private copy in our cloud. It's never used for anything else, never used for advertising, and never shared or sold. You can remove access in Health Connect's settings.
  • Food search and barcode lookup. When you search for a food by name (including when checking a recipe's ingredients), or scan or type a product barcode, the words you typed or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts to look up products and their nutrition. Nothing else is sent with them. Scanning uses Google's on-device barcode scanner, which doesn't need camera permission.
  • Recipe links. When you add a recipe from a link (or share a page to the app), your phone downloads that page from the recipe website to read the ingredients, just as a browser would. Nothing else is sent, and we never see which recipes you look at.
  • Obsidian. If you use the Obsidian version, data is saved in your own vault's plugin folder.

Live sync between devices (optional)

If you choose Live sync in Settings → Your data → Sync between devices, the app keeps your Quartermaster data in step across your phones and tablets within seconds. It works like this:

  • Your account. You sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication (run by Google on our behalf). Firebase holds your Google email address and account id. If you also use household or friends groups, it's the same account.
  • What's stored. A copy of your Quartermaster data, the same as a backup: meals and what you've logged, recipes, stock, food at home, meal plans and shopping, schedule, projects, habits, workouts, swims, weight, goals, your history (including daily steps, distance, sleep, weight and workouts filed from Health Connect), mood check-ins and settings. Settings that belong to one device stay on it and aren't stored: reminder times, the widget theme, text size, the Health Connect and Google Calendar connections, and Household & friends.
  • Where. Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in Google's London data centre (europe-west2), stored under your account. It's encrypted on the way and where it's stored.
  • Who can read it. Only you, signed in on your own devices. The database's security rules don't let anyone else read or change it, including other people in your groups. We don't use it for anything except syncing your devices: it is never used for advertising, never analysed, and never shared or sold.
  • Offline. Changes you make offline are kept on the phone and sync when you're back online.
  • Turning it off. Choosing "Only on this phone" (or Drive sync) first brings in the latest from your other devices, then asks whether to keep the cloud copy (so your other devices carry on) or delete it. Deleting it stops live sync on your other devices too; each keeps the data already on it.
  • Deleting your account deletes the cloud copy as well (see below).

Household & friends groups

This is optional. If you create or join a group, these things are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, run by Google on our behalf) so everyone in the group sees the same thing:

  • the name you choose to show in groups, and a colour;
  • what you add to the group: chores (how often they repeat, which chore follows on from which, and if one's put off until tomorrow), who is doing them and when (if you plan or assign one), and who did them, the shared shopping list, joint projects and their tasks, recipes you share with the group (the recipe itself: name, portions, ingredients with amounts and nutrition, method, notes and web address, never how often you've cooked it), and the points they earn;
  • if "Share my weekly numbers" is on for that group: this week's counts of workouts, distance, swims, step-goal days and 1 Thing days, and the same counts for each of the last 31 days so group challenges (such as "most swims in October") can be scored. Nothing else about your health, meals or weight is shared;
  • challenges you set in a group: the title, what's being counted, the dates and any target.

Sending a recipe with Send… uses your phone's share sheet: it goes only to the app and people you choose, and never through us.

By default you don't sign in for this. Firebase gives the app an anonymous id so it can tell members apart.

Linking to Google (optional). If you tap "Link to Google" (Settings → Your data, or on the Home tab after a reinstall), your anonymous id is linked to your Google account using Firebase Authentication, so you can sign in again on a new install and get your groups back. Firebase then holds your Google email address and account id, and we store a small profile that only you can read: your group name, colour and the list of groups you're in. It isn't used for anything else. "Delete my sharing account" in Settings → Your data leaves your groups and deletes the profile, the anonymous id, the Google link and, if you use it, your live sync copy.

Only members of a group can read it, and you join only with a code someone in the group gives you. Turning off "Share my weekly numbers" deletes your numbers from that group. "Leave group" removes you from it; chores and items you added stay for the others. To have everything you added to a group deleted, email us with the group and the name you used in it.

Suggestions and "Help improve Quartermaster" (both optional)

  • Suggest as a preset. When you tap the lightbulb on a habit or saved recipe and confirm, that one habit's name and settings, or that recipe's name, portions, ingredients with grams, macros and web address, are sent to us so we can consider making it a ready-made preset. Nothing you've logged goes with it, and it isn't linked to your name.
  • Help improve Quartermaster. Off unless you turn it on in Settings → Your data, where you can see exactly what would be sent. Once a week it sends: the app version; the names and types of your habits; the names, portions and website of your saved recipes; which features you have switched on; and a few counts (such as how many days you logged in the last four weeks, and how many habits, recipes, batch meals and groups you have). It is stored under a random id that isn't linked to you or your groups. It never includes your meals, weight, health data, schedule or anything you've logged. Turning it off deletes what was sent.

Both are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), can't be read back by the app, and are only used to improve Quartermaster's presets and features.

Reporting misuse

If someone in a household or friends group posts something inappropriate, or you don't know who they are: the person who created the group can remove them (the × next to their name), and anyone can tap Report a problem at the bottom of the group's card, or email balisestudio.dev@gmail.com. We'll look into it and can remove content or people from a group.

Deleting your account and data

Quartermaster doesn't need an account. The only account is the optional one behind household and friends groups and live sync (an anonymous id, plus your Google email if you linked it or turned on live sync).

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my account (under Sync between devices) or Delete my sharing account. Either removes you from your groups and deletes your live sync copy, your profile, your anonymous id and any Google link straight away. Nothing saved on the phone is touched.
  • Without the app (for example if you've uninstalled it): email balisestudio.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Quartermaster account" and the Google email you linked, or the name you used in your groups. We'll delete your account, your live sync copy, your profile and group membership within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Chores, shopping items and other things you added to a group stay for the other members unless you ask us to delete them too.
  • Data saved on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Erase my data" in Settings.
  • "Help improve Quartermaster" data is deleted when you turn it off. Preset suggestions aren't linked to you, so they can't be traced back to you.

Feedback and crash reports

"Send feedback", and "Send report" on the screen you'd see if the app hit a bug, open an email in your own email app, addressed to us. It's filled in with the app's build number and your phone model, and a crash report also includes the error message. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your phone: when rest between gym sets is over, and any reminders you switch on in Settings (for example to log a meal or close the day). They're scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file of your data and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes (Drive, Files, email…) is up to you. We never receive it.

Daily backup to Google Drive (optional). If you turn it on in Settings → Your data, the app signs in with your Google account and saves a copy of the same backup file, once a day, in a "Quartermaster backups" folder in your own Google Drive, keeping the last 14. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which only lets the app see and change the files it created itself, not anything else in your Drive. The file goes directly from your phone to your Drive; we never receive it, and it isn't used for anything else or shared with anyone. "Restore from Drive" reads one of those files back. Turning it off stops new backups; the files already saved stay in your Drive until you delete them. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sync between devices through Google Drive (optional). If you choose it in Settings → Your data → Sync between devices, the app keeps your Quartermaster data in step across your own phones and tablets through a single file, quartermaster-sync.json, in the same "Quartermaster backups" folder in your Google Drive, using the same drive.file permission. It holds the same data as a backup, except settings that belong to one device (reminder times, the widget theme, text size, Health Connect, Google Calendar and Household & friends), which stay on that device. The file goes directly between your devices and your Drive; we never receive it, there is no account with us, and it isn't used for anything else or shared with anyone. Choosing "Only on this phone" stops syncing on that device; the file stays in your Drive for your other devices until you delete it. The same Google API Services User Data Policy terms above apply.
