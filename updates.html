What's new in Quartermaster

Balise Studio · Newest first · Getting started guide

Repeats your way, work hours and planning ahead Build 57 · 3 October 2026

  • Habits can repeat several times a day, a set number of times a week or month, every few days, weeks or months, or on a date each month.
  • Chores repeat the same ways. A "twice a week" chore stays in the list until it's done twice.
  • Not today: put a habit or chore off until tomorrow without breaking your streak.
  • Chore follow-ups, like Empty the dishwasher after loading it, appear when they're ready, with a reminder.
  • Projects add up the time of their tasks, and project tasks can wait on another task.
  • Set your work hours in Settings → Schedule: chores, habits and other tasks are fitted around work, and work tasks inside it. You can still put anything anywhere yourself.
  • Plan meals and shopping for 2 weeks, the month or up to a date, and pick any date when planning.
  • Auto-fill uses things up: recipes that share fresh ingredients with what you've just cooked come first.

A proper recipe book Build 56 · 3 October 2026

  • Tag recipes for breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks: auto-fill uses them after your batch stock, cooking once and planning the leftovers, within your calorie target.
  • Say how each recipe's leftovers keep (freezes well, keeps 2 days, best fresh) and auto-fill plans accordingly.
  • Make a recipe from scratch, searching for ingredients or scanning the packs you've got.
  • Google Calendar no longer asks you to sign in again out of the blue.
  • Record a batch straight from one of your recipes. Food search on Open Food Facts is fixed on Android.
  • Recipes now live in Plan → Meals: search by name or ingredient, and sort by recent, most cooked or protein.
  • Tap any ingredient to fix it: search your foods, the basics or Open Food Facts, or create your own. Quartermaster remembers the fix next time.
  • Change amounts in grams, spoons, tins or each, add or remove ingredients, and see what each one adds.
  • The method comes in from the website, with room for your notes. Duplicate a recipe to make a version of it.

A Later list, and chores on your day Build 52 · 3 October 2026

  • Later: a list for tasks with no day yet. Add one from + → Task → Later, or move a task there from its edit screen. Start Day offers them each morning.
  • Plan a chore onto your day, with a time if you like. It goes on your schedule, and ticking it there ticks it off for the household.
  • Give a chore to someone else in the household. Everyone sees who's doing it and when, in that person's colour.
  • Start Day now lists today's chores, with Add to my day.

Workouts from your watch, and editing tasks Build 51 · 2 October 2026

  • Workouts saved in Health Connect (runs, rides, walks, gym sessions and swims) now appear in Quartermaster on their own. Allow Workouts in Settings → Health Connect.
  • Tap a task's name on Today, in Plan or in Start Day to rename it, change its time or how long it takes.
  • The + button is tidier: food first, a "done it or plan it" choice, and tasks can go straight into a project on a chosen day.
  • Coming up shows the next 7 days, including project tasks and household chores.
  • Pick your own colour on the Home tab, and Distance trends only shows what you actually do.

Ready for your feedback Build 48 · 1 October 2026

  • Send feedback in one tap from the What's new card or Settings → Help. Your build and phone are filled in.
  • Whoever made a group can remove a member, and every group has a Report a problem link.
  • The Home tab is now called Household & friends at the top.
  • Recipes: sprigs, leaves, pinches and knobs now get sensible weights.

Challenges, one shopping list and keeping your groups Build 44 · 1 October 2026

  • Group challenges such as "most swims in October", with a finish line, an optional target and a winner.
  • One shopping list: your batch-cook list and your household's list together.
  • Put a recipe straight onto a day's meal plan as you save it.
  • Link to Google (optional) to keep your groups if you reinstall.
  • Suggest a habit or recipe as a preset, and optional "Help improve Quartermaster".

Your week, and a bit of polish Build 43 · 1 October 2026

  • Monday recap: your week, and who won each group. Fireworks if it's you.
  • Ticks now pop with a little buzz, and progress bars glow when you hit a goal.
  • Friendlier empty screens that show what to do first.
  • Easier to read and tap, plus Reduce motion in Settings → Display.

Sides, recipe portions and leftovers 1 October 2026

  • Plan sides with a meal: lasagne plus a side salad and garlic bread.
  • Plan a portion of a saved recipe and say how many you're cooking for. The shopping list scales to match.
  • Cooked for guests? Add the leftovers to your fridge or freezer.
  • Log a planned meal and its sides in one tap.

Recipes from the web 1 October 2026

  • Add recipes from a link: tap + → Recipe, or Share → Quartermaster from Chrome.
  • Say how many portions you're making and the ingredients scale to match.
  • Save + shopping list adds the amounts to your list, and you can plan the cook for a day.
  • Cooked it? The batch form opens ready filled.

Meals moves into Plan 1 October 2026

  • Meals now sits inside the Plan tab, next to Schedule and Projects, and the + button is back in the middle.
  • Smoother swiping between tabs: the page follows your finger and the bottom bar stays still.
  • Optional: swipe round in a loop (Settings → Display).
  • A new Help section in Settings, and a What's new card after each update.

Household & friends 30 September 2026

  • New Home tab: share chores (with rotas and points), joint projects and a shopping list with your household.
  • Weekly leaderboards with friends: workouts, distance, swims and more.
  • Join with a six-letter code. Only what you put in a group is shared.

Food search, reminders and calendar fixes 30 September 2026

  • Search foods by name, plus recent foods and "same as yesterday".
  • Sugars, saturated fat, salt and fibre with UK traffic lights.
  • Goal weight with a forecast, and optional gentle reminders.
  • Remove blocks from your schedule. Deleting an app event in Google Calendar now removes it here too.

First test builds September 2026

  • Today: calories, protein, water and swim tiles, habits with streaks, Today's 1 Thing and a focus timer.
  • Plan: your day's schedule from Google Calendar, planning sessions and batch cooks ahead, and projects.
  • Meals: a weekly meal plan with auto-fill, fridge and freezer batch stock, defrost reminders and a shopping list.
  • Health: swims, gym sessions with personal bests and a rest timer, distance, Health Connect steps, sleep and weight.
  • Home-screen widgets, shortcuts and backups.
