What's new in Quartermaster

Balise Studio · Newest first · Getting started guide

Your week, and a bit of polish Build 43 · 1 October 2026

  • Monday recap: your week, and who won each group. Fireworks if it's you.
  • Ticks now pop with a little buzz, and progress bars glow when you hit a goal.
  • Friendlier empty screens that show what to do first.
  • Easier to read and tap, plus Reduce motion in Settings → Display.

Sides, recipe portions and leftovers 1 October 2026

  • Plan sides with a meal: lasagne plus a side salad and garlic bread.
  • Plan a portion of a saved recipe and say how many you're cooking for. The shopping list scales to match.
  • Cooked for guests? Add the leftovers to your fridge or freezer.
  • Log a planned meal and its sides in one tap.

Recipes from the web 1 October 2026

  • Add recipes from a link: tap + → Recipe, or Share → Quartermaster from Chrome.
  • Say how many portions you're making and the ingredients scale to match.
  • Save + shopping list adds the amounts to your list, and you can plan the cook for a day.
  • Cooked it? The batch form opens ready filled.

Meals moves into Plan 1 October 2026

  • Meals now sits inside the Plan tab, next to Schedule and Projects, and the + button is back in the middle.
  • Smoother swiping between tabs: the page follows your finger and the bottom bar stays still.
  • Optional: swipe round in a loop (Settings → Display).
  • A new Help section in Settings, and a What's new card after each update.

Household & friends 30 September 2026

  • New Home tab: share chores (with rotas and points), joint projects and a shopping list with your household.
  • Weekly leaderboards with friends: workouts, distance, swims and more.
  • Join with a six-letter code. Only what you put in a group is shared.

Food search, reminders and calendar fixes 30 September 2026

  • Search foods by name, plus recent foods and "same as yesterday".
  • Sugars, saturated fat, salt and fibre with UK traffic lights.
  • Goal weight with a forecast, and optional gentle reminders.
  • Remove blocks from your schedule. Deleting an app event in Google Calendar now removes it here too.

First test builds September 2026

  • Today: calories, protein, water and swim tiles, habits with streaks, Today's 1 Thing and a focus timer.
  • Plan: your day's schedule from Google Calendar, planning sessions and batch cooks ahead, and projects.
  • Meals: a weekly meal plan with auto-fill, fridge and freezer batch stock, defrost reminders and a shopping list.
  • Health: swims, gym sessions with personal bests and a rest timer, distance, Health Connect steps, sleep and weight.
  • Home-screen widgets, shortcuts and backups.
